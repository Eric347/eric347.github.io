This Privacy Policy explains how Eric Games Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile games.

Games Covered by This Policy

Hexa Smart Sort

A hexa sorting puzzle game supported by advertisements. Hexa Smart Sort does not currently offer the Bolt IQ ad-free purchase system.

Bolt IQ

A puzzle game supported by advertisements, with optional permanent or monthly ad-free purchases through Google Play.

Information Stored on Your Device

Our games may store gameplay information locally on your device so they can remember progress and preferences. Depending on the game, this may include:

  • Level progress, scores, and tutorial completion;
  • Coins, rewards, and unlocked or equipped game items;
  • Music, sound, vibration, and notification preferences;
  • Advertising frequency progress; and
  • For Bolt IQ, verified ad-free purchase or subscription status.

This local information is not a public player profile. It can generally be removed by clearing the game's storage or uninstalling the game, subject to purchase records retained by Google Play.

Advertising

Hexa Smart Sort and Bolt IQ are ad-supported. They may use advertising services including Google AdMob, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and advertising partners made available through those platforms.

Advertising services may process information such as:

  • Device type, operating system, language, and app version;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • Advertising identifiers, when available and permitted;
  • Ad impressions, clicks, rewards, and other ad interactions; and
  • Diagnostics, performance data, and fraud-prevention signals.

This information may be used to deliver ads, measure performance, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize ads. Players can use Android and Google privacy or advertising controls to manage available choices.

In Bolt IQ, purchasing ad-free access disables automatic interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available when a player chooses to use them. Hexa Smart Sort is currently supported by ads and does not use Bolt IQ's ad-free purchase system.

Bolt IQ In-App Purchases

This section applies only to Bolt IQ. Bolt IQ offers optional purchases through Google Play, including permanent ad-free access and a monthly ad-free subscription. Google Play processes payment information. We do not receive or store full credit card or bank account details.

To confirm that a purchase is valid, Bolt IQ sends the product ID, application ID, transaction ID, and Google Play purchase receipt or token to our purchase-verification service hosted with Firebase and Google Cloud. The service checks the purchase with Google Play before enabling the purchased benefit.

Server and Security Information

Bolt IQ's purchase-verification service may process an IP address and basic request or error logs for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and rate limiting. We do not use that service to create advertising profiles or player accounts.

How Information Is Used

Information described in this policy may be used to:

  • Operate each game and remember progress and settings;
  • Show and measure advertisements;
  • Provide optional rewards connected to rewarded ads;
  • For Bolt IQ, verify purchases and restore eligible entitlements;
  • Protect our games and services from fraud and abuse; and
  • Diagnose errors and maintain reliable service.

Third-Party Services

Third-party providers process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play, Google AdMob, Firebase, and Google Cloud)
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads)
  • Firebase Privacy and Security Information (Bolt IQ purchase verification)

Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the game is uninstalled. Advertising providers retain information according to their own policies. Google retains purchase and payment records according to its policies and legal obligations.

Bolt IQ's verification service does not intentionally store purchase receipts in a player database, although limited technical logs may be retained by service providers for security and operational purposes.

Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Use Android and Google privacy or advertising controls;
  • Decline optional rewarded ads;
  • Clear locally stored game data or uninstall a game;
  • For Bolt IQ, purchase ad-free access if available; and
  • Contact us regarding an applicable privacy request.

Our games do not currently create remote player accounts. Most gameplay data can therefore be deleted directly from the device. Google Play purchase records are controlled by Google and may need to be managed through your Google account or Google Play support.

Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing is subject to their privacy policies and applicable data-protection safeguards.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including encrypted HTTPS communication for Bolt IQ purchase verification. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our games, services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
