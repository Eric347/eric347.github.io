Bolt IQ Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 13, 2026

Bolt IQ does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location. The game uses advertising and offers optional in-app purchases.

This Privacy Policy explains how Eric Games Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Bolt IQ mobile game ("Bolt IQ" or the "Game").

Information Stored on Your Device

Bolt IQ stores game information locally on your device so the Game can remember your progress and preferences. This may include:

  • Level progress and tutorial completion;
  • Coins, daily rewards, and unlocked or equipped game items;
  • Music, sound, vibration, and notification preferences;
  • Advertising frequency progress; and
  • Your verified ad-free purchase or subscription status.

This local information is not a public profile. You can remove it by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling the Game, subject to any purchase records retained by Google Play.

Advertising

Bolt IQ uses Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads to provide interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. These advertising services and their partners may process information such as:

  • Device type, operating system, language, and app version;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • Advertising identifiers, when available and permitted;
  • Ad impressions, clicks, rewards, and other ad interactions; and
  • Diagnostics, performance data, and fraud-prevention signals.

This information may be used to deliver ads, measure performance, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize ads. Purchasing ad-free access disables automatic interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available when you choose to use them.

In-App Purchases

Bolt IQ offers optional purchases through Google Play, including permanent ad-free access and a monthly ad-free subscription. Google Play processes payment information. We do not receive or store your full credit card or bank account details.

To confirm that a purchase is valid, the Game sends the product ID, application ID, transaction ID, and Google Play purchase receipt or token to our purchase-verification service hosted with Firebase and Google Cloud. The service checks the purchase with Google Play before enabling the purchased benefit.

Server and Security Information

Our purchase-verification service may process an IP address and basic request or error logs for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and rate limiting. We do not use the purchase-verification service to create advertising profiles or player accounts.

How We Use Information

We use information described in this policy to:

  • Operate the Game and remember your progress and settings;
  • Show and measure advertisements;
  • Provide optional rewards connected to rewarded ads;
  • Verify purchases and restore eligible entitlements;
  • Protect the Game and purchase system from fraud and abuse; and
  • Diagnose errors and maintain reliable service.

Third-Party Services

The following providers process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads)
  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play, Firebase, and Google Cloud)
  • Firebase Privacy and Security Information

Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the Game is uninstalled. Google retains purchase and payment records according to its own policies and legal obligations. Our verification service does not intentionally store purchase receipts in a player database, although limited technical logs may be retained by our service providers for security and operational purposes.

Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Use your Android and Google privacy or advertising controls;
  • Decline optional rewarded ads;
  • Purchase ad-free access to disable automatic interstitial ads;
  • Clear local Game data or uninstall the Game; and
  • Contact us regarding an applicable privacy request.

Bolt IQ does not currently create a remote player account. Therefore, most gameplay data can be deleted directly from your device. Google Play purchase records are controlled by Google and may need to be managed through your Google account or Google Play support.

Children's Privacy

Bolt IQ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing is subject to their privacy policies and applicable data-protection safeguards.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including encrypted HTTPS communication for purchase verification. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
