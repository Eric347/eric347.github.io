Information Stored on Your Device

Our games may store gameplay information locally on your device so they can remember progress and preferences. Depending on the game, this may include:

  • Level progress, scores, and tutorial completion;
  • Coins, rewards, achievements, goals, and unlocked game items;
  • Music, sound, vibration, and notification preferences;
  • Advertising frequency progress; and
  • For Bolt IQ, purchase, subscription, or entitlement status.

Advertising

Hexa Smart Sort and Bolt IQ are ad-supported. They may use advertising services including Google AdMob, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and other advertising partners available through those platforms.

Advertising services may process information such as device type, operating system, language, app version, IP address, approximate location, advertising identifiers where available, ad impressions, clicks, rewards, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals.

In Bolt IQ, purchasing ad-free access disables automatic interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available when a player chooses to use them.

Bolt IQ Pre-registration Reward Terms and Conditions

Reward: Eligible players who pre-register for Bolt IQ on Google Play may receive a Bolt Overdrive reward in the game after launch.

These Terms and Conditions apply to the Bolt IQ pre-registration reward offered by Eric Games Studio.

1. Eligibility

  • You must pre-register for Bolt IQ through Google Play before the game is released in your region.
  • You must install and open Bolt IQ after the game becomes available.
  • The reward is available only where Bolt IQ pre-registration and the game are available through Google Play.
  • The reward is limited to eligible Google Play users and may require the same Google account used for pre-registration.

2. Reward Details

The pre-registration reward is a Bolt Overdrive in-game item or power-up. Bolt Overdrive is a gameplay reward for use inside Bolt IQ only. It has no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money, transferred, sold, or redeemed outside the game.

3. Delivery of Reward

The reward may be delivered automatically after Bolt IQ launches and the player opens the game. Delivery may depend on Google Play systems, internet connection, app version, account eligibility, and successful reward configuration.

4. No Guarantee of Availability

We will make reasonable efforts to provide the reward to eligible players. However, the reward may be delayed, changed, replaced with a similar in-game reward, or unavailable if required by technical issues, platform rules, fraud prevention, abuse, or legal requirements.

5. Fair Use and Abuse

We may refuse, remove, or adjust a reward if we believe it was claimed through abuse, fraud, technical manipulation, duplicate claims, or a violation of Google Play rules or these terms.

6. Changes to These Terms

We may update these pre-registration reward terms if the game, reward, Google Play requirements, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page.
